Spenza – Personal and Family Finance Tracker

Spenza is a personal and family finance tracking application available on Android and web. Track daily expenses, manage accounts and net worth, monitor debts and EMIs, set budgets and spending limits, and view dashboard insights. Designed for individuals and families.

How Spenza uses your Google account data

  • Google Drive App Data (drive.appdata): Used exclusively to store a private backup of your own financial data in a hidden app-specific folder (appDataFolder). This folder is not visible in Google Drive and is never shared with anyone.
  • Google Sheets (spreadsheets): Used only when you choose to import existing records from a spreadsheet you own into Spenza. Spenza does not read your spreadsheets automatically — only on explicit user action.
  • Google Sign-In: Used to authenticate you securely. Your email is never used for marketing.
