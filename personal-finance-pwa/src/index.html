Spenza

Telecom Expense Management

Spenza is a telecom expense management platform that helps businesses manage, optimize, and control their communication costs. Track usage across all lines and plans, monitor invoices, identify savings, and gain full visibility into your organization's telecom spend — all in one secure place.

Features

  • Expense tracking: Monitor telecom invoices and usage across all accounts and plans.
  • Cost optimization: Identify unused lines, overage charges, and savings opportunities.
  • Dashboards & insights: Visualize spend trends and get actionable reports for your team.
  • Budgets & alerts: Set spending limits and receive alerts before costs exceed thresholds.

How Spenza uses your Google account data

Spenza only requests the Google permissions it needs to back up and import your own financial data. Your data stays yours — Spenza never sells it or shares it for advertising.

Google Drive — app data folder (scope: drive.appdata)

Used only to store a private backup of your own financial data in a hidden, app-specific folder in your Google Drive. This folder is not visible alongside your normal Drive files, is not human-readable, and is never shared with anyone. Spenza cannot see, read, or touch any of your other Drive files with this permission — only the backup it created.

Google Sheets (scope: spreadsheets)

Used only when you choose to import existing records into Spenza from a spreadsheet you own. This is optional and user-initiated — Spenza does not read spreadsheets in the background and does not access spreadsheets you have not explicitly selected.

Basic profile & email (scopes: openid, email, profile)

Used to sign you in securely and identify your account. Spenza does not use your email for marketing.
